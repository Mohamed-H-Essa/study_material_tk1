On a day = am. am Montag, am Dienstag, am Wochenende — am is an dem contracted, so it is dative. Every day of the week is der, which is why it is always am and never an der.
Times of day as adverbs end in -s and mean "in the …, habitually": morgens (in the mornings), mittags (at midday), nachmittags (in the afternoons), abends (in the evenings), nachts (at night). Morgens macht Emma Yoga.
Verb-second still rules. When a time expression starts the sentence, the verb stays in slot two and the subject moves behind it: Am Montag macht Emma Yoga. · Abends geht sie ins Restaurant. Never Am Montag Emma macht …
sich treffen is reflexive: Sie trifft sich mit einer Freundin. = she meets up with a friend. You can also drop the reflexive and take an object: Sie trifft eine Freundin.
Two words for friend. eine Freundin can be a female friend or a girlfriend; ihr Freund (with a possessive) usually means her boyfriend. Context decides — Emma ist verliebt, sie hat einen Freund.
