16 · der / die / das

The gentle intro: every noun has a gender, and the adjective changes shape too (ein roter Stift, eine rote Schere, ein rotes Buch).

rainbow = bonus beyond the video

The core ideaein → der/die/das, and the adjective agrees

derder Stift · die Stifteein roter Stift — masculine, adjective ends ‑er
diedie Schere · die Schereneine rote Schere — feminine, adjective ends ‑e
dasdas Buch · die Bücherein rotes Buch — neuter, adjective ends ‑es
Words from the video: der Löffel (spoon), die Tasse (cup), das Messer (knife), das Handy (phone), die Hand, der Tisch (table). Opposites: groß/klein, alt/neu, schmutzig/sauber, rund/eckig.
Why bother? Getting the gender right is what lets you form the rest of the sentence later (Dativ/Akkusativ). Learn the article with every noun, never alone.
