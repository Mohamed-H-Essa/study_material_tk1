19 · Fragen stellen

Two ways: flip the verb to the front (yes/no), or start with a W-word. Builds directly on the position-2 rule.

rainbow = bonus beyond the video

The two patterns

Yes/no question: put the conjugated verb first.
Du kochst selber. → Kochst du selber?
W-question: W-word first, then verb (position 2), then the rest.
Was kochst du? · Wann kommst du nach Hause?
Object questions: with welche / wie viele, the object rides along right after the W-word:
Wie viele Bier hat Janosch noch im Kühlschrank?

W-words

werwhowaswhat
wowherewannwhen
wiehowwarumwhy
woherfrom where (toward me)wohinto where (away from me)
welchewhichwie vielehow many
woher vs wohin (bonus depth): Wohin gehst du? = movement away. Woher kommst du? = movement toward the speaker.
