29 · Was man im Schnee machen kann

What you can do in the snow: throw snowballs, build a snowman, make a snow angel, go sledging, skate. Grammatically this is the man kann … lesson — the modal verb können sending its partner verb to the end of the sentence.

man kann + infinitive at the END. This is the modal-verb rule and it is everywhere in this video: Mit dem Schnee kann man einen Schneemann bauen. · Man kann Schlitten fahren. The conjugated modal takes slot two; the other verb goes last, unchanged.
man = "one / you (in general)" — not the noun der Mann. It always takes the er/sie/es verb form: man kann, man legt sich, man bewegt. It is the normal way German states what people generally do.
Weather verbs use an empty es: Es schneit. (it is snowing) · Es ist kalt. There is no real subject — es is just holding the slot, exactly like English "it".
Snow compounds — all from der Schnee: der Schneeball (der Ball), der Schneemann (der Mann → plural die Schneemänner), die Schneeballschlacht (die Schlacht = battle). Gender and plural always come from the final part.
Directions with the body: von oben nach unten (from top to bottom), von links nach rechts (from left to right). Pattern: von … nach …, both with the plain adverb, no article.
