67 · Präpositionen mit Akkusativ

Five little words that always take the Akkusativ — no thinking, no deciding, no exceptions. Learn them as a set and five whole categories of sentence become automatic.

rainbow = bonus beyond the video

The setFive words, always Akkusativ

durch — through  · durch den Park
für — for  · für den Vater
gegen — against  · gegen den Wind
ohne — without  · ohne den Schlüssel
um — around / at (time)  · um den Tisch
The mnemonic. Say them in this order until it is a single word: durch-für-gegen-ohne-um. Five syllables, one breath. Teachers have used this chant for decades for a reason — it turns a decision into a reflex. When any of these five appears, you stop deciding: it is Akkusativ, always.
Why a chant beats a rule here. These prepositions never change their case — unlike the two-way ones you will meet in lesson 72. So there is genuinely nothing to work out. Chanting a closed set is the cheapest possible way to make it automatic, and it frees your attention for the parts that do need thought.
