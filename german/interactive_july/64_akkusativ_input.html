64 · Wer macht es — wirklich?

No new grammar in this lesson. This is training. Every question below is built so that the only way to answer it is to actually read the article. That is the whole point — and it is the part that 59 lessons of listening could not give you.

rainbow = bonus beyond the video

Why this lesson existsMaking the ending necessary

When you hear ich sehe den Hund, you do not need den — the meaning is obvious without it. So your brain skips it. Perfectly sensible, and it is why the endings never stuck.

Below, both answers are always equally possible. A dog can see a cat; a cat can see a dog. The article is the only information that decides it. Read it, or you cannot answer.
Expect these to feel slow and slightly annoying. You will want to answer from the word order and you will have to stop yourself. That resistance is the habit being rewritten. Go slowly — accuracy matters far more than speed here.
