30 · Weihnachtsvokabeln

German Christmas, concretely: the Adventskalender with 24 little doors, Lebkuchen and Plätzchen, decorating the tree, the presents on the 24th, and the Weihnachtsmann with his sleigh. Culturally loaded vocabulary you will hear every December.

-chen makes things small — and always neuter. die Tür → das Türchen (a little door on the calendar) · der Platz → das Plätzchen (a biscuit). The root vowel usually takes an umlaut: Platz → Plätzchen. Same rule as das Mädchen and das Mäppchen.
The passive appears a lot here — things get done, without saying by whom: Es wird viel Schokolade gegessen. (a lot of chocolate is eaten) · Es werden viele Plätzchen gebacken. Form: werden + past participle. Just recognise it for now.
Germans open presents on the evening of the 24th, not the morning of the 25th: Am Abend vom 24. Dezember kommen die Geschenke unter den Weihnachtsbaum. The Weihnachtsmann brings them — in some regions it is the das Christkind instead.
Decorating takes mit + dative: Wir schmücken den Tannenbaum mit Weihnachtskugeln. Note the dative-plural -n on Kugeln — dative plurals almost always end in -n.
Movement takes the accusative: Die Geschenke kommen unter den Weihnachtsbaum. (they move to under the tree — accusative). Compare where they then lie: Die Geschenke liegen unter dem Baum. (dative). That is lesson 35 in miniature.
