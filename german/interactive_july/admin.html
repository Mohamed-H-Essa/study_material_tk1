⚙ Admin

Control what each account sees and override a lesson's ✓ done state.

Visible = the lesson shows on that person's hub. Turning it off hides the card from them entirely (their progress is kept, never deleted — flip it back on and it returns).
Done = force the ✓ mark on or off for that person. Use this when a check-off passed but the sync lagged. It writes straight into their normal progress, so their devices pick it up on the next sync.
Your own account always sees everything — those toggles are locked on purpose.
