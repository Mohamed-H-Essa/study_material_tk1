Three strengths of opinion: Ich liebe den Sommer. (love) → Ich mag den Sommer. (like) → Ich hasse Kaugummi. (hate). All three take the accusative: der Sommer → ich liebe den Sommer.
Loving to do something uses es + zu + infinitive: Ich liebe es, im Sommer in die Berge zu gehen. In casual speech you will also hear the plain infinitive as a noun: Ich liebe Wandern. or Ich wandere gern.
gern is the everyday way to say you like doing something — just add it to the verb: Ich esse gern Pizza. (I like eating pizza) · Ich wandere gern. The opposite is nicht gern: Ich esse nicht gern bei McDonald’s.
gefallen flips the sentence around. Das Video gefällt mir. literally = the video pleases me → "I like the video". The thing liked is the subject, the person is in the dative: Wenn euch das Video gefallen hat … — the phrase every one of these videos ends on.
der Berg → die Berge, but "into the mountains" is in die Berge (accusative — movement) while "in the mountains" is in den Bergen (dative — location, and note the extra -n that dative plurals always take).
